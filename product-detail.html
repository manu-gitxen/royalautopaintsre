STEP 01
Surface Preparation

Ensure basecoat has fully flashed off (15-20 min @ 20°C). Degrease surface with HB Body 770 Antisil Degreaser using clean microfiber cloth.

STEP 02
Precision Mixing

Mix thoroughly according to calibrated mixing cup ratios. Strain using 125-micron paint filter directly into gravity spray gun cup.

STEP 03
Application & Cure

Apply in spray booth with recommended airflow. Allow 10 min flash-off before baking at 60°C for 25 minutes or IR curing for 12 minutes.

Safety Regulations & Storage Conditions

For professional use only. Always wear approved dual-cartridge respirators (A2P3), safety glasses, and chemical-resistant nitrile gloves during mixing and spray application. Store in original sealed containers between 10°C and 30°C away from direct sunlight, open flames, and moisture. Guaranteed shelf life of 24 months from manufacturing date when sealed.
