Why Work With Us?

We foster technical excellence, continuous factory training, and career progression in Doha's vibrant automotive, marine, and industrial surface finishing industry.

Factory Brand Training

Direct certified technical training on HB Body (Greece) and Challenger (Axalta) computerized color mixing, clearcoats, and paint chemistry.

Modern Lab & Tools

Work with high-accuracy digital spectrophotometers, multi-angle daylight simulation booths, and automated spray paint aerosol filling lines.

Stable & Trusted

Career Growth

Structured promotion paths from Junior Technician to Senior Color Master, Technical Field Specialist, or Key Account Sales Lead.

Full Time Colorimetry Lab

Senior Color Mixing & Formulation Technician

Wakalat St (Street 23), Industrial Area, Doha
Role Responsibilities:
  • Formulate accurate automotive paint mixes using digital spectrophotometer and manufacturer formula databases.
  • Operate computerized dispensing scales and high-speed mechanical agitation mixers.
  • Perform spray test-cards and multi-angle metamerism verification under daylight standard booths.
  • Maintain batch records, tinting adjustments, and pigment inventory replenishment logs.
Key Requirements:
  • 3+ years hands-on experience in automotive refinish paint mixing (solvent-borne / 2K systems).
  • Familiarity with European systems (HB Body, Challenger, PPG, Sikkens, or equivalent).
  • Valid Qatar ID / transferable visa or NOC preferred.

B2B Technical Sales Executive — Automotive & Marine

Doha & Greater Qatar Regional Markets
Role Responsibilities:

Full Time Field Engineering

Spray Booth & Equipment Maintenance Specialist

Street 23 & Client Bodyshop Sites across Qatar
Role Responsibilities:
  • Install, calibrate, and service commercial paint spray booths, heating burners, and exhaust ducting systems.
  • Inspect and replace multi-stage ceiling filters, pocket filters, and paint stop floor filtration media.
  • Perform maintenance and precision calibration on professional HVLP/RP spray guns and air compressors.
  • Provide on-site technical support to ensure compliance with Qatar civil defense safety standards.
Key Requirements:
  • Technical diploma or vocational training in mechanical/electrical or HVAC maintenance.
  • Prior hands-on experience with industrial spray booths or ventilation systems.
  • Ability to troubleshoot burner ignition, air flow balance, and thermostatic controls.
Apply via WhatsApp Email CV
Full Time Logistics & Supply

Warehouse & Paint Inventory Logistics Coordinator

Street 23 Central Logistics Warehouse, Doha
Role Responsibilities:
  • Oversee inbound container shipments, container unstuffing, and inventory recording of paints and hardeners.
  • Maintain temperature-controlled, hazardous materials (HAZMAT) storage protocols in compliance with civil defense.
  • Coordinate daily order picking, packing, and fleet dispatches to workshops and clients across Doha.
  • Conduct periodic stock audits and monitor re-order levels for fast-moving clear coats and thinners.
Key Requirements:
  • 2+ years experience in warehouse operations, preferably in paints, automotive spares, or chemicals.
  • Basic computer literacy (stock ERP, Excel spreadsheets, email dispatch).
  • Physically fit, detail-oriented, and safety-conscious.
