FAQS

Your Most Important Questions Answered Here

How accurate is your computerized automotive paint color matching?

We utilize advanced multi-angle spectrophotometers and Challenger OEM formulation databases. By analyzing physical vehicle samples (such as a fuel door), we measure metallic flake, pearl intensity, and sun-fade to produce an exact color match every single time.

What paint mixing ratios should be used in Qatar's summer heat?

For high ambient temperatures in Qatar, we recommend using Challenger Slow or Extra Slow hardeners and reducers mixed according to technical data sheet ratios (typically 2:1 by volume). This ensures proper flow, leveling, and prevents premature flash-drying or solvent popping.

Can you prepare custom automotive paint in aerosol spray cans?

Yes, we offer on-site custom aerosol filling for any car paint code or custom color. Each 400ml aerosol can is filled with professional automotive-grade paint and fitted with a precision fan nozzle, ideal for spot repairs and small panels.

Do you offer on-demand delivery for bodyshops and garages in Doha?

Yes, Royal Auto Paints provides fast, reliable delivery directly to garages, workshops, and dealerships across Street 23, Industrial Area, and all of Doha. We ensure your refinish supplies and mixed batches arrive right on schedule.

What abrasives and surface prep are recommended before painting?

Proper preparation is essential for durable paint adhesion. We supply premium abrasives from P80 to P3000, along with wax and grease removers, anti-static wipes, and high-build 2K primers to prepare smooth, flawless repair surfaces.
