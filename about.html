How We Are Different

Engineered on Quality, Precision, & Guaranteed Results

We never have any compromise in the quality of products that we sell to our customers — always supplying the best and guaranteed formulations.

Direct Factory Sourcing (Zero Mediators)

We are the authorized dealers of Axalta Challenger made in Germany and HB Body from Greece. We purchase all products directly from the manufacturer’s factory to our store without any mediator, so we can offer authentic European quality at the best price with the latest global technology.

100% FACTORY DIRECT

Inspect & Ensure Quality Prior to Purchase

We allow our customers to thoroughly check and ensure formulation quality before purchasing from us. With full batch certificates of analysis (COA), test spray-out validation cards, and digital colorimetric readouts, you receive complete confidence in every order.

VERIFIED BEFORE PURCHASE

All-Round Support & Technical Services

We provide comprehensive support and technical assistance after every purchase. We are here with you at any time, anywhere across Qatar for help, spray gun calibration, paint booth filtration, and on-site formula mixing consulting.
