Workflow Methodology

The Precision Refinish Pipeline

A rigorous 4-step workflow ensuring zero color mismatch, flawless atomization, and prompt workshop delivery across Doha.

STEP 01

Color Code &
Spectro Scan

We analyze OEM paint code plates and use calibrated digital spectrophotometers on the physical vehicle sample to measure weathering and metallic flake orientation.

STEP 02

Computerized
Micro-Dispensing

Our computerized tinting dispensers weigh microscopic pigment formulations down to 0.01g, blending pearls, metallics, and toners to exact global formulas.

STEP 03

Spray-Out &
Custom Packaging

Test spray-out cards verify color fidelity under 6500K daylight simulation lamps. Formulations are securely packaged into tins or pressurized aerosol cans.

STEP 04

Fast Bodyshop
Dispatch

Same-day courier dispatch to automotive workshops, dealerships, and industrial facilities across Doha and the Qatar Industrial Area.
